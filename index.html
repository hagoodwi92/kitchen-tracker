Day-one rule

Portion it now

  • The second you're home, split the pack into meal-size bags — don't leave that decision for later in the week.
  • Fridge: only what you're cooking in the next 2-3 days.
  • Freezer: everything else, flat in bags, dated.
🎃 Sam's rule applies here too: always check what's in the bag before you trust it — an undated freezer bag is basically an unchecked piece of candy.
